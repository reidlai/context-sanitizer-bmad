# Digest D2-R1-1: Selective Context, Self-Information & RECOMP / Submodular Pruning

## Findings (Claims)

1. **Claim:** *Selective Context* (Yucheng Li, Bo Dong, Chenghua Lin, Frank Guerin, EMNLP 2023, *"Compressing Context to Enhance Inference Efficiency of Large Language Models"*, `arXiv:2310.06201`) computes self-information $I(x_t) = -\log_2 P(x_t \mid x_0, \dots, x_{t-1})$ using a **causal language model** (such as GPT-2, OPT, or LLaMA) to score and prune redundant tokens, phrases, or sentences—not a zero-weight static lookup.
   - **Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06201 / https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.391/
   - **Publisher:** ACL Anthology (EMNLP 2023) / arXiv
   - **Pub Date:** 2023-10 / 2023-12
   - **Accessed:** 2026-10-21
   - **Confidence:** high
   - **Class:** method / limitation

2. **Claim:** *RECOMP* (Fangyuan Xu, Weijia Shi, Eunsol Choi, ICLR 2024, *"RECOMP: Improving Retrieval-Augmented LMs with Context Compression and Selective Augmentation"*, `arXiv:2310.04408`) uses two **trained neural compressors**—an extractive dual-encoder sentence selector trained with contrastive learning and an abstractive fine-tuned T5-Large generator—and does **not** use submodular chunk selection or zero-weight BM25 pruning.
   - **Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.04408 / https://openreview.net/forum?id=mlJLVigNHp
   - **Publisher:** OpenReview (ICLR 2024) / arXiv
   - **Pub Date:** 2023-10 / 2024-01
   - **Accessed:** 2026-10-21
   - **Confidence:** high
   - **Class:** method / limitation
